## 1. Pairwise contracts

| Pair | Contract |
|---|---|
| Remembers ↔ Signal | Remembers reads aggregate demand; it never manipulates matches toward Director goals |
| Remembers ↔ Wayfarer | world changes make personal knowledge stale; that tension is exploration |
| Remembers ↔ PACT | public contracts, treaties, mercenary deals become high-confidence history (no AI inferring politics from chat) |
| Remembers ↔ Ledger | big campaigns and notorious bounties become history; regional politics shift witness behaviour; never reveal targets |
| Remembers ↔ Crown Law ↔ Ledger | Bounty Endowments fund Writs; Amnesty stops new Writs; Caravan Protection funds escorts |
| Signal ↔ Wayfarer | "you hunted with X three times" as an opt-in soft signal, never a hidden ranking |
| Signal ↔ PACT | Signal introduces; PACT commits (crafter → Crafting Pact; mercenaries → Mercenary Contract; supplier → Purchase Order) |
| PACT ↔ Ledger | share hardened escrow primitives (Asset Service), never institutions: Notarius / Magistrate / Broker |
| Wayfarer ↔ Ledger | clues kept with confidence and expiry; a hunt's end never leaves a tracking database |
| Citizens ↔ everything | citizens use every system through the protocol, under the same rules; actor class affects policy tables only |
| Chronicle ↔ everything | reads publication DTOs only |

## 2. An H5 story (what the systems produce together)

*Genesis week 6. Testers have been in Aden for nine days.*

**Days 1–7 — pressure.** The Silver Wharf house's spoilers strip the Forge of the Gods for Durable Metal Plate. Aden Remembers
sees extraction above the family budget; yield share drifts to the Valley of Saints within bounds. Rune merchants start buying
metal plate; a tavern rumor mentions "strange hauls coming out of the Valley".

**Day 8 — discovery.** A tester's spoiler notices in their Wayfarer journal that Valley yields beat their memory of the Forge.
They tell their clan, quietly. Their journal records it as KNOWN; their clanmates' as TOLD.

**Day 10 — leakage.** Their private store in Rune sells too much plate. Citizens notice; the Iron Covenant's out-of-band channel
lights up. Signal shows elevated demand for spoilers near Rune (coarse, player-generated).

**Day 12 — conflict.** Iron Covenant moves a party into the Valley. PvP. Regional conflict rises; the Gazette's Roads section
notes a Rune caravan delayed by fighting.

**Day 14 — institution.** The testers' clan, outnumbered, uses Signal to find mercenaries — a human Destroyer's clan and the
Ash Wolves (citizens for hire). They sign a Mercenary Contract at the Rune Castle Notarius: 30M, defend the Valley approach
two evenings, measurable participation. Registered public, terms sealed. The crier announces it.

**Day 15 — the Ledger.** Iron Covenant's leader buys a Black Contract on the testers' clan leader. The hunter (alias *Black
Feather*) questions the Rune gatekeeper in person, buys a verified sighting from a low-level citizen who saw the mark near the
Valley, and closes in. The mark's friendly warehouse keeper mentions a stranger asking questions; *"You feel watched."*
The mark switches to walking routes through Hunters Village.

**Day 17 — failure.** Black Feather ambushes, loses to the mark's bodyguard. A Torn Black Seal drops: value band 25–50M, origin
Rune network, sponsor sealed. Reverse investigation begins: the seal points to the Rune Broker; an informant's payment trail
points to an Iron Covenant treasurer.

**Day 20 — politics.** The testers publish the evidence through their own channels. No AI declares guilt. Their alliance declares
clan war on Iron Covenant and registers for the next Territory War against Rune.

**Week 4 — consequences.** Rune's lord (Iron Covenant ally) enacts a Bounty Endowment against the testers' clan's chaotic
members; the testers' clan answers with a Treaty offer to a neutral house, forfeit escrowed. Caravans reroute around the
war. Broker fees have removed 6M Adena from circulation. The Valley of Saints has become strategic.

**Month 3 — history.** The Chronicle promotes "The Valley War": contract dates, Territory War results, the failed assassination,
the treaty, the trade shift. The bard in Rune sings it. A monument stands in Rune. The saga replay of the decisive Territory
War is on the website. Nothing was scripted.
